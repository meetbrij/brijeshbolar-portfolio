Problem Statement

The Hidden Cost of Deal Prep

In investment banking, junior analysts spend a disproportionate share of their time on low-leverage preparation tasks: pulling comparable transaction data, assembling pitch deck sections, summarising target company profiles, formatting financial models. This is work that requires attention to detail and domain knowledge — but not senior judgment.

The result: senior bankers are bottlenecked on review, junior analysts are burned out on repetitive assembly, and deal teams move slower than they should at the most time-sensitive moments of a transaction.

The AI Deal Room targets this gap — not replacing judgment, but automating the assembly layer so analysts can focus on insight.

Pain Points Targeted
  • Manual comparable transaction research (3–5 hrs per deal)
  • Target company profile assembly from disparate sources
  • Pitch deck section drafting and reformatting
  • Deal timeline and milestone tracking across teams
  • Document version management in live deal rooms
Primary Persona
Junior IB Analyst (Year 1–3)
High domain knowledge, limited time. Responsible for the analytical backbone of deal materials but constrained by manual process overhead.
Solution

What the AI Deal Room Does

⚡
Smart Deal Setup
Upload documents once. AI extracts deal thesis, key metrics, and risks — turning a 200-page CIM into a structured briefing in seconds, not hours.
📋
AI-Generated Prep Sheets
Structured briefing documents auto-generated before every client meeting — deal context, open issues, and talking points assembled without any manual effort.
🎙️
Meeting Intelligence
Paste a transcript, get summaries, sentiment signals, and red flags instantly. Converts raw meeting notes into structured intelligence the whole deal team can act on.
✅
Action Item Tracker
Auto-assigned, prioritised, and deadline-tracked action items extracted from meeting outputs — so nothing falls through the cracks between client touchpoints.
🏦
IC Memo Drafter
A near-complete Investment Committee memo assembled section by section from all deal signals — the analyst refines and signs off, the AI handles the first draft.
