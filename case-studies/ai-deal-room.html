02 Problem Statement

The Hidden Cost of Deal Prep

In investment banking, junior analysts spend a disproportionate share of their time on low-leverage preparation tasks: pulling comparable transaction data, assembling pitch deck sections, summarising target company profiles, formatting financial models. This is work that requires attention to detail and domain knowledge — but not senior judgment.

The result: senior bankers are bottlenecked on review, junior analysts are burned out on repetitive assembly, and deal teams move slower than they should at the most time-sensitive moments of a transaction.

Pain Points Targeted
Manual Assembly at Deal Speed
  • Manual comparable transaction research consuming 3–5 hours per deal
  • Target company profile assembly from disparate sources (CIM, Bloomberg, internal databases)
  • Pitch deck section drafting and reformatting for every client meeting
  • Deal timeline and milestone tracking spread across emails and spreadsheets
  • IC memo drafting from scratch — the most time-consuming single deliverable
Primary Persona
Junior IB Analyst (Year 1–3)
  • High domain knowledge, severely limited time across multiple live deals
  • Responsible for the analytical backbone of all deal materials
  • Constrained by manual process overhead on every deliverable
  • No dedicated tool — relies on Word, Excel, email threads, and memory

Deal Intelligence View — Project Everest Every deal gets a structured intelligence page: deal thesis, financial snapshot, and AI-flagged risk signals. The Risk Flags panel highlights the issues that matter most — FDA inspection outcomes, key-man risk, competitor moves — so analysts aren't buried in documents to find them.

03 Core Features

Smart Deal Setup & AI Company Snapshot

The first two modules handle the hardest part of deal prep: getting context into the system quickly. Upload once, and the AI extracts structured intelligence — deal thesis, financials, risk signals — so the analyst starts informed, not from a blank page.

Module 1
Smart Deal Setup
  • Structured form captures deal codename, type, estimated value, and sector
  • Document upload (CIM, teaser, financials) triggers AI extraction pipeline
  • AI populates deal context, key metrics, and initial risk flags automatically
  • Deal is instantly available to the full team — no manual briefing required
Module 2
AI Company Snapshot
  • LLM-generated company brief with confidence scoring on each insight
  • Key financials (Revenue, EBITDA, CAGR, Deal Size) extracted and formatted
  • Watch-outs flagged automatically — environmental notices, concentration risks, timeline risks
  • Stage Readiness panel shows outstanding checklist items before advancing the deal

New Deal Setup Three-step form: deal details → company information → document upload. AI begins extraction as soon as documents are uploaded.

AI Company Snapshot Generated from the uploaded CIM. Confidence scores on each insight tell the analyst where to verify. Watch-outs surface issues the document buries.

04 Meeting Intelligence

Pre-Meeting Prep & Post-Meeting Intelligence

Two modules bracket every client interaction. Before the meeting: a structured AI-generated prep sheet so the analyst walks in fully briefed. After the meeting: paste the transcript and get structured intelligence — decisions, commitments, concerns, open questions — that the whole deal team can act on.

Module 3
AI-Generated Prep Sheets
  • Auto-generated before every client meeting from accumulated deal signals
  • Deal context section: seller objectives, sensitivities, watch-outs, comparable transactions
  • Confidence scoring on each section — high/medium/low with source attribution
  • Regenerate any section instantly as new information arrives
  • Exportable to PDF for print or sharing with senior bankers
Module 4
Meeting Room Intelligence
  • Paste transcript → AI extracts Summary, Key Decisions, Client Concerns, Commitments, Open Questions
  • Tabbed interface: analysts drill into the specific type of intelligence they need
  • One-click action: "Generate Action Items" turns commitments into tracked tasks
  • Multiple transcripts per deal — all analysis saved and accessible in context

Pre-Meeting Prep Sheet AI-assembled from deal metadata and uploaded documents. Deal context, seller sensitivities, and comparable transactions — all sourced and confidence-scored.

Meeting Room Transcript analysed into structured tabs. The mandate discussion summary captures verbal agreements, timeline commitments, and fee structure — ready for the team in seconds.

05 Workflow Automation

Action Item Tracker

Nothing falls through the cracks. Action items are auto-extracted from meeting outputs, assigned to team members, prioritised, and deadline-tracked — so deal teams don't need a separate project management tool for a live transaction.

Module 5
Auto-Populated Action Items
  • Action items extracted directly from meeting transcript analysis — zero manual entry
  • Auto-assigned to deal team members based on role and context
  • Priority flagging (High/Medium/Low) and due date set from meeting commitments
  • Status tracking: Open → In Progress → Done, with overdue alerts
  • Source attribution: every action links back to the meeting that generated it
Design Decision
No Manual Entry Required
  • The tracker is a by-product of meeting intelligence — not a separate workflow
  • Analysts never have to context-switch to a project management tool mid-deal
  • Filter by status, assignee, priority, or source meeting
  • Designed for deal velocity: fast to scan, fast to update, zero overhead

Action Items — Project Falcon Eight items auto-extracted from the mandate discussion. High-priority tasks like obtaining audited financials and reviewing the Westbridge shareholders agreement are flagged immediately, with owners, due dates, and source attribution visible at a glance.

06 IC Memo Drafter

AI-Assembled Investment Committee Memo

The IC memo is the single most time-consuming analyst deliverable in any M&A process. The AI Deal Room assembles a near-complete first draft section by section — sourced from all uploaded documents and meeting summaries — so the analyst edits and refines rather than drafting from scratch.

How It Works
Source-Attributed Draft, Section by Section
  • Each section (Executive Summary, Company Overview, Financial Analysis, Risk Assessment) generated independently with source citations
  • Confidence scoring per section — analysts know where to verify before senior review
  • Inline comments and annotations: analysts add context without altering the AI draft
  • Regenerate any section as new information is added to the deal
  • Watermarked as AI-generated — full transparency on every page
Design Principle
AI Drafts. Banker Decides.
  • No section is submitted without human review — workflow enforces this
  • "Submit for IC Review" button requires acknowledgment that all sections have been verified
  • Low-confidence sections flagged for mandatory senior review before submission
  • Export to DOCX for seamless handoff into existing bank document workflows

IC Memo — Executive Summary AI-generated from deal metadata and meeting summaries. Source attribution shown per paragraph. Draft clearly watermarked — human review required before submission.

IC Memo — Recommendations Structured IC recommendation with deal thesis, conditions precedent, and next steps. Ready for senior banker review and submission to the Investment Committee.

07 Conclusion

What This Demonstrates

The AI Deal Room is a proof that the assembly layer of investment banking — the part that consumes junior analyst hours without requiring senior judgment — can be automated without removing human accountability from the process.

Every design decision enforces the same principle: AI does the first draft, the banker owns the final. Confidence scoring, source attribution, mandatory review gates, and AI watermarking are not compliance features — they are the trust architecture that makes the tool usable in a regulated, high-stakes environment.

Built in under a week using Claude Code, the prototype covers the complete deal lifecycle across five modules. It demonstrates not just a product concept, but a repeatable approach to AI-first tooling in professional services: start with the workflow, identify the assembly bottleneck, automate the draft, preserve the judgment.
