Problem Framing

The Trust Gap in Wealth Management

India's wealth management industry is structurally broken — not because of a product gap, but a trust gap. Traditional relationship managers earn trail commissions on product placement, not client outcomes. Portfolios are accidental collections of overlapping funds, generating ₹1–2 lakh per year in hidden commissions on a ₹1 crore portfolio.

The supply-side crisis compounds this: there are roughly 1,000 wealthy families per qualified RM in India. High-quality, conflict-free advice doesn't scale with humans alone.

Dezerv's insight: make mis-selling visible before the commercial conversation begins — and then solve it structurally through a discretionary mandate and fixed-fee model.

The Status Quo Problem
  • Hidden trail commissions of ₹1–2L/yr on ₹1 Cr portfolios
  • Portfolios with 30+ overlapping funds — accidental, not intentional
  • RM incentive: maximize AUM, not client returns
  • No accountability — RMs change, portfolios don't
  • Geography gap: quality advice concentrated in Tier 1 cities
Dezerv's Structural Fix
  • Direct-to-client model — no broker intermediaries
  • Discretionary mandate: Dezerv owns outcomes, not just advice
  • Fixed fee: 1% flat or 10% performance above high-watermark
  • Earn only when clients earn
  • Dual-app funnel converts trust at scale
Product Architecture

How the Platform Works

📱
Dual-App Funnel
Wealth Monitor (free, 700K users, ₹2.5L Cr tracked) makes mis-selling visible — users see their hidden commissions and portfolio overlaps. This qualifies leads organically.

Dezerv App (KYC-gated, 6,568 PMS clients, ₹17,000 Cr AUM) converts them into managed clients with discretionary mandates.
📊
Conflict-Free Product Stack
Three tiers by client size:

IPA — ₹50K minimum, trail commission model for mass affluent

PMS — ₹50L minimum, 1% or 10% performance fee

AIF Cat I/II/III — ₹1 Cr+, management fee + carry for UHNI
🤖
AI Strategy (3 Layers)
Now: Internal tools reducing non-client-facing RM activity to near zero — same headcount, dramatically more clients served.

Near-term: Voice agent for client check-ins and portfolio queries.

2026+: Agentic systems for portfolio rebalancing and tax optimisation at scale.
Strategic Insights

What Makes This Model Work

🛡️
Trust as a Product Feature
Dezerv doesn't market trust — they engineer it. The conflict-free fee structure, discretionary mandate, and SEBI-filed performance records make trust verifiable, not a promise. Zero SEBI complaints across 6,568 PMS clients is the product working as designed.
🔄
Flywheel: Performance → Referrals → AUM
Every equity strategy beats its benchmark (Alpha Focus: +3.68% alpha, SEBI-filed). Clients who stay 12 months add 40–50% more capital in Year 2 and refer peers — a compounding growth flywheel that reduces CAC over time as AUM scales.
📍
Unoccupied Market Quadrant
Dezerv occupies a quadrant no one else holds: institutional-grade + digitally-native + conflict-free. Traditional PMS providers lack digital distribution. Robo-advisors lack human touch and discretionary depth. This positioning is structurally hard to replicate.
