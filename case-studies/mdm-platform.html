Overview

The Problem & The Solution

Problem
A 30–40% STP Failure Rate
  • 30–40% of new financial instruments failed Straight-Through Processing on the first attempt
  • 5+ data sources reconciled manually per instrument — Bloomberg, Reuters, internal databases
  • No standardized review interface for risk reviewers
  • Zero end-to-end audit trail — creating compliance exposure and trade booking delays
  • Dual personas (DataOps Analyst and Risk Reviewer) working in silos with no shared workflow
Solution
AI-Assisted 6-Stage Governance Workflow
  • Unified platform connecting DataOps Analysts to Risk Reviewers in a single tracked workflow
  • AI layer: STP score diagnostics, missing field detection, similar instrument recommendations
  • Live STP readiness scoring with conflict resolution across 5+ data sources
  • Immutable audit log with full attribute lineage for regulatory compliance
  • Dual-control governance: analyst submits → reviewer approves, no self-certification

Product Thinking

Key Design Decisions

👥
Dual-Persona Architecture
Separated DataOps Analyst (discovery, enrichment, validation) from Risk Reviewer (inspection, approval, certification) into distinct views with appropriate permissions. No self-certification allowed — enforcing the four-eyes principle from day one.
🤖
AI-Assisted Enrichment
Instead of replacing analysts, the AI layer acts as a co-pilot: flagging anomalies, detecting missing fields, and recommending validated configurations from 156+ comparable instruments. This preserves analyst judgment while eliminating repetitive lookup work.
📋
Immutable Audit Log
Every attribute change, override, and approval decision is captured in a timestamped, tamper-proof audit trail with mandatory reason logging for reviewer overrides. This wasn't just a compliance checkbox — it became the trust layer that allowed faster approvals.
